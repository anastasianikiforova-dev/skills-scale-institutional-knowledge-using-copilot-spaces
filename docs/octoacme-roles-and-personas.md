# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance (QA) Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and execute testing strategies, establish quality gates, and ensure deliverables meet acceptance criteria before release. They work closely with developers and product managers to ensure quality standards are met throughout the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans and test cases
- Define acceptance criteria and quality gates for releases
- Manage test environments and automation frameworks
- Identify and track defects and quality risks
- Approve releases based on quality metrics
- Collaborate with developers on testability and quality design

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality software with minimal production defects
- Reduce cycle time through efficient testing
- Establish confidence in product reliability
- Minimize support burden through comprehensive testing

### Typical Communication
- Weekly quality metrics reviews with Project Managers
- Test results and defect reports to Developers
- Quality gates approval in release planning meetings
- Test strategy documentation and automation frameworks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with **Developers** to define testable acceptance criteria and review test coverage
- Coordinates with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and go/no-go decisions
- Reports to **Project Managers** on quality status and release readiness

---

## Tech Lead / Solution Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads provide technical direction, design guidance, and architectural decisions to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical strategy. They mentor the team and ensure technical excellence throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architecture proposals
- Provide guidance on technology choices and trade-offs
- Mentor developers and promote technical best practices
- Identify technical risks and propose solutions
- Ensure solutions integrate with existing systems
- Guide technical decisions during planning and execution

### Goals
- Maintain technical excellence and consistency across projects
- Minimize technical debt and architectural issues
- Enable scalable and maintainable solutions
- Build team capability through mentoring and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions with Developers
- Technical guidance in planning sessions and code reviews
- Risk assessments and technical trade-off analysis
- Technical architecture documentation and decision records

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Guides and mentors **Developers** on technical decisions and best practices
- Partners with **Project Managers** on technical feasibility and risk identification
- Collaborates with **Product Managers** on technical trade-offs impacting product decisions

---

## Release Manager / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, manage release schedules, monitor production health, and enable reliable delivery to customers. They ensure smooth transitions from development through production operations.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain deployment pipelines and infrastructure
- Coordinate release schedules and deployment windows
- Monitor production systems and resolve incidents
- Manage rollback and recovery procedures
- Document deployment procedures and runbooks
- Manage dependencies and coordination with operations teams

### Goals
- Enable reliable, frequent releases to production
- Minimize downtime and production incidents
- Ensure smooth handoff from development to operations
- Maintain system performance and stability

### Typical Communication
- Release notes and deployment runbooks with Developers
- Production metrics and incident reports to Project Managers
- Deployment coordination meetings with stakeholders
- Post-deployment validation and health checks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with **Developers** on deployment procedures and production support
- Works with **Project Managers** on release scheduling and stakeholder communication
- Partners with **QA Leads** on testing in production-like environments and release validation

---

## Executive Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide business oversight, strategic alignment, and decision authority for major initiatives and escalations. They ensure projects align with organizational strategy and remove high-level blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project charter and major changes
- Resolve escalations and remove organizational blockers
- Communicate status to leadership and board
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy
- Make decisions on scope, priority, and resource allocation

### Goals
- Maximize business value delivery from projects
- Ensure projects align with company strategy and goals
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and alignment
- Enable efficient escalation resolution

### Typical Communication
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Project status reports and escalations
- Strategic planning and budget reviews
- High-level decisions on scope and priority changes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides strategic direction and approvals to **Project Managers**
- Reviews business metrics and outcomes with **Product Managers**
- Resolves escalations from all team members during execution challenges

---

## Customer Success / Support Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Success teams represent customer needs, feedback, and support perspective in project planning and quality decisions. They ensure delivered solutions meet real customer needs and minimize support burden.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and communicate customer feedback and needs
- Identify support-related risks and gaps in solutions
- Validate solutions from customer perspective
- Assist with rollout communication and training materials
- Monitor customer adoption, satisfaction, and support trends
- Provide customer perspective in planning and quality reviews

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions meet customer needs and expectations
- Reduce support burden and customer friction
- Drive product adoption and customer satisfaction
- Identify improvement opportunities based on customer feedback

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and feature requests
- Support ticket patterns, trends, and pain points
- Rollout readiness assessments and customer communication plans
- Customer validation on design and quality decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides customer perspective to **Product Managers** on priorities and features
- Works with **Project Managers** on rollout plans and customer communication
- Collaborates with **QA Leads** on customer-facing quality scenarios
- Advises **Tech Leads** on customer-impacting technical decisions

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The personas work together in various project phases: QA Leads ensure quality, Tech Leads guide architecture, Release Managers coordinate deployment, Executive Sponsors provide oversight, and Customer Success teams ensure customer alignment.
- When discussing risks, escalations, or decisions in scenarios, reference the appropriate personas for realistic role-based interactions.
